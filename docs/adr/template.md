# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem or force makes a decision necessary? State the constraints (desk workflow,
numerical accuracy, operability, maintenance cost) and any facts a reader needs. Be neutral:
describe the situation, not the solution.

## Decision

What is being done, stated in full sentences and in the active voice ("We will ..."). Include
enough detail that a contributor can tell whether a given change complies.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what new obligations arise. List negative
consequences and accepted risks as plainly as the positive ones.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious alternative, with the reason it was not chosen.
